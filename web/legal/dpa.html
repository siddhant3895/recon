Data Processing Agreement (DPA) & Confidentiality

Effective Date: 10 November 2026 · Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India)

1. Purpose & Parties

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") governs the processing of financial records, bank statements, and accounting ledgers provided by the Chartered Accountancy firm ("Data Controller") to Recon ("Data Processor").

2. Scope of Processing & Non-Disclosure

The Data Processor shall process uploaded records strictly and exclusively for the purpose of executing the deterministic reconciliation prep engine and generating reconciliation schedules. Data shall not be shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party, nor utilized for generalized artificial intelligence model training.

3. Data Residency within India

All uploaded files, databases, cached tokens, and audit events shall reside and remain strictly within the geographical boundaries of the Republic of India (Amazon Web Services Region: ap-south-1, Mumbai). At no time shall client financial records transit through foreign cloud regions.

4. Mandatory 30-Day Purge Policy

All raw PDF bank statements and CSV ledger files uploaded by the Data Controller shall be permanently unlinked and wiped from storage within thirty (30) calendar days of processing via an automated purge job. An immutable record of each purge event shall be logged in the Data Controller's audit log.

5. Security Safeguards

The Data Processor implements AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, per-firm database Row-Level Security (RLS), and short-lived pre-signed storage URLs with a maximum validity of 15 minutes.

6. Incident Notification SLA

In the event of a verified or suspected security breach affecting Controller data, the Data Processor shall notify the designated managing partner via email and telephone within seventy-two (72) hours of verification.

7. Guardian Co-Signature & Legal Capacity

To ensure total commercial and legal enforceability under the Indian Contract Act, 1872, all formal agreements entered into with CA firms are formally co-signed and guaranteed by an adult legal guardian representing Recon.
