Golden Dataset Composition

The golden benchmark is a synthetic, seeded dataset of 300 labelled pairs (449 ledger lines) built to exercise realistic ledger messiness. It is not a sample of real bank data, and results on your firm's files will differ:

  • Indian Payment Rails: NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, and UPI narration patterns with invoice references.
  • 40 Deliberately Adversarial Cases in four labelled categories:
    • Split UPI settlements — many-to-one Tier 4 groups (40 groups in total).
    • Decoy entries — near-identical amounts and dates from the wrong party.
    • OCR/typo noise — reference numbers with digit-level corruption.
    • Ambiguous multi-reference narrations and multi-account statement lines.

What Recon Does Not Catch (By Design)

We state our boundaries plainly so firms are never surprised:

  • Scanned or Mobile Camera PDFs: In v1, if a PDF contains fewer than 100 characters per page, Recon rejects it with an explicit message rather than hallucinating text.
  • Entries Outside Time/Amount Windows: Transactions with date mismatches beyond 30 days or amount deltas beyond 2% are refused by hard gates.
  • Complete Narration & Reference Shifts: If both the reference number and party narration are completely different between bank and ledger, the row routes to unmatched.
  • Accounting Judgments: Contra-entries, bad debt write-offs, or complex tax adjustments require human CA discretion.
