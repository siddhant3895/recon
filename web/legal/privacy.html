Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 10 November 2026 · DPDP Act Compliant

1. Data Minimization Principle

We collect only the minimal information required to deliver the reconciliation preparation service: contact details of the subscribing CA firm (name, firm name, business email) and the transaction records uploaded for reconciliation.

2. No Sale or Third-Party Monitization

Recon does not sell, rent, or monetize firm or client transaction data. We do not use financial transaction details or ledger records to train generative AI foundation models.

3. Regional Storage & Purge

All uploaded financial records are hosted exclusively within the AWS ap-south-1 Mumbai region. Raw statement files are permanently deleted 30 days after upload completion.

4. Contacting the Privacy Officer

For inquiries regarding data deletion, access rights, or audit exports under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, please contact our privacy compliance desk at privacy@recon-prep.in.
