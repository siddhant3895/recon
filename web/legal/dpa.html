Data Processing Agreement (DPA) & Confidentiality

Draft v0.1 · 11 November 2026 · Never executed with anyone. Not legal advice.

1. Purpose & Parties

This template governs how a Chartered Accountancy firm ("Data Controller") would provide financial records, bank statements, and accounting ledgers to a future hosted Recon service ("Data Processor"). It is written to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, India) and must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before use.

2. Scope of Processing & Non-Disclosure

Records would be processed strictly and exclusively for running the deterministic reconciliation engine and generating schedules — never shared with or sold to third parties, and never used to train generative AI models.

3. Data Residency

[To be negotiated.] A hosted deployment would commit to processing and storing controller records exclusively within India. Today Recon is software you run yourself, so residency is determined by where you operate it.

4. Retention & Purge

[To be negotiated.] The draft intent: raw uploaded statements and ledgers are purged within an agreed window (e.g. 30 days) using the retention job built into Recon, with an audit record of each purge outcome (purged / skipped / failed).

5. Security Safeguards

[To be negotiated.] A hosted deployment would need to commit to specific safeguards — encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, and access controls. The current prototype has none of these (see the Security page), so no such representation is made here.

6. Incident Notification

[To be negotiated.] The draft intent: notify the designated managing partner of any verified breach within an agreed SLA. No monitoring or notification pipeline exists today.

7. Guardian Co-Signature & Legal Capacity

Because the developer is a minor, any agreement actually entered into would be co-signed by an adult parent or legal guardian, as required for enforceability under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

Status

This document is a starting point for a future agreement. No firm is currently bound by it, and no hosted Recon service exists to be a party to it.
