Terms of Service

Last Updated: 10 November 2026

1. Nature of the Service

Recon is a computational reconciliation preparation software layer. Recon does NOT file statutory tax returns, issue audit certificates, or provide chartered accountancy advice. All reconciliation decisions, classifications, and filings are the sole professional responsibility of the qualified human user.

2. Human Verification Requirement

Recon does not include any automatic acceptance mechanism. Matches are proposed with explainable confidence scores. It is a material condition of using the service that an authorized reviewer independently inspects and confirms all matches before exporting data into filing software.

3. Billing & Row Metering

Subscriptions are metered strictly by processed bank statement rows per monthly statutory cycle. Unused row allocations do not roll over. Accounts exceeding row limits will be invited to upgrade or billed for overage at contractual rates. No per-seat fees apply.

4. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, Recon and its operators shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or statutory penalty damages arising from tax filing delays, bank PDF formatting variances, or unreviewed export errors.
