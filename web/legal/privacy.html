Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 11 November 2026 · DRAFT for lawyer review

1. What This Prototype Actually Collects

The only personal data the demo server accepts is the optional interest form on the "Shadow Month" page (your name, firm name, and email address). That submission is held in the memory of the running process and written to its in-memory audit log. It is not saved to a database, not emailed to anyone, and is lost when the process restarts.

2. Transaction Data

Reconciliation runs happen on your own machine, via the command line, on files you supply. The demo on this site uses synthetic sample data only. Wherever Recon is run, output files are written to that machine's local disk under your control.

3. No Sale, No Trackers, No AI Training

We do not sell, rent, or monetize data, run advertising trackers, or use financial records to train generative AI models. The site's pages use no third-party scripts or analytics.

4. Contact

There is no formal privacy desk yet. For any data question about a deployment you run, contact the developer via the details on the About page.

5. Status of This Document

This is a draft written to align with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not constitute legal advice or a compliance certification.
