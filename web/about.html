About Recon & The Builder

Why a student researcher in Nagpur decided to solve mechanical typing in Indian chartered accountancy firms.

Watching CA Firms Drown Every Month

In Nagpur, like every commercial city in India, the second and third weeks of every month are chaos for chartered accountants. Ahead of the 11th (GSTR-1) and the 20th (GSTR-3B), senior partners and junior articles spend 15 to 25 hours a week doing pure manual data entry: comparing bank PDFs, tracing UTR numbers, finding split transactions, and typing them into Tally.

These are qualified professionals with deep financial and tax acumen, spending prime working hours performing the work of a mechanical keyboard.

Addressing "You're 15"

When CA firm partners first hear that the software architect is a Class 10 student, the natural reaction is skepticism: "Can a teenager build software robust enough for our firm's statutory audits?"

Here is the honest answer:

  • Algorithmic Foundation: Having published a peer-reviewed computational physics preprint, my background is discrete numerical modeling, optimization algorithms, and bounded combinatorics. Reconciliation is not a tax law problem—it is a bounded graph and subset-sum matching problem.
  • No "AI Autopilot" Gimmicks: Many adult founders build flashy wrappers around generative LLMs that hallucinate numbers or leak data to US APIs. Recon rejects LLMs in the matching path. It uses 100% deterministic, explainable mathematics.
  • Adult Co-Signatures: Recon is a prototype with no commercial contracts in force today. Any future contract, NDA, or DPA would be co-signed by an adult guardian, as required under Indian contract law.
  • Proof Before Promises: You do not need to take my word for it. The demo runs the real engine on labelled synthetic data, and every published metric comes from a reproducible report you can generate yourself.

Our Core Engineering Rules

1. Deterministic & Reproducible

Same input + same normalizer version = byte-identical output, verified by regression tests.

2. Explainability Over Cleverness

Every candidate match must show exactly why it was proposed before the article accepts it.

3. Never Auto-Accept

The software does the typing; the qualified human makes the decision.

4. Local, Cloud-Free Processing

Recon makes zero network calls — data stays on the machine running it, wherever you choose to run it. A built-in retention job purges raw files past their expiry.
