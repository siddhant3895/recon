Terms of Service

Last Updated: 11 November 2026 · DRAFT for lawyer review

1. Nature of the Software

Recon is prototype reconciliation preparation software. It is not a hosted subscription service, does NOT file statutory tax returns, issue audit certificates, or provide chartered accountancy advice. All reconciliation decisions, classifications, and filings are the sole professional responsibility of the qualified human user.

2. Human Verification Requirement

Recon does not include any automatic acceptance mechanism. Matches are proposed with explainable confidence scores. It is a material condition of using the service that an authorized reviewer independently inspects and confirms all matches before exporting data into filing software.

3. Billing & Row Metering (Proposed)

No paid service is being sold today, so no billing terms are in force. If a paid service launches, the draft intent is metering by processed bank statement rows per monthly cycle, with no per-seat fees. Any such terms would be agreed in an executed contract, co-signed by the developer's legal guardian (the developer is a minor).

4. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, Recon and its operators shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or statutory penalty damages arising from tax filing delays, bank PDF formatting variances, or unreviewed export errors.

5. Governing Law

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. The courts of Nagpur, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
