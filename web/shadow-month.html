The Shadow Month Idea

Before asking any firm to change its process, Recon should prove itself running alongside manual reconciliation for one monthly cycle. The pilot described below is a plan, not a running program — today you can register interest and try the public demo on synthetic data.

Register Your Firm's Interest

Fill out these three fields. What actually happens today: the running server records your submission in memory and in its audit log. This prototype sends no email and stores nothing in a database — if the process restarts, the record is gone. Your details are used only to gauge pilot interest, never sold or shared.

Planned Pilot Design (Not Yet Built)

How a pilot would work if/when Recon becomes a hosted, secured service:

1

Agreements & Isolated Environment

A bilateral NDA and DPA would be executed (co-signed by the developer's legal guardian, since the developer is a minor), and a dedicated, isolated instance provisioned with real authentication and encryption — none of that infrastructure exists yet.

2

Parallel Runs with Your Team

During the month, your statements and ledgers would be processed by Recon while your team performs its standard manual work, using the same keyboard-first review flow you can try in the demo.

3

Measured Accuracy & Throughput Report

At the end of the cycle, a comparison report of Recon's proposals vs. your team's decisions would document precision, false positives, and time saved. This reporting tooling is not built yet — it is part of the pilot plan.
