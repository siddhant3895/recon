How It Works in Three Steps

1

1. Ingest

Upload digital bank statement PDFs or CSVs and your client's Tally/Zoho ledger export. Amounts convert to exact paise, dates normalize, and Indian banking tokens (UTR, NEFT, IMPS, RTGS, UPI) extract deterministically.

2

2. Keyboard Review

A keyboard-driven worklist proposed by a 4-tier cascade. High-confidence matches (≥95%) pre-focus for rapid Enter-Enter-Enter acceptances. Ambiguous rows show component score breakdowns and human explanations.

3

3. Clean Export

Download structured XLSX and CSV reconciliation schedules containing matched pairs, unmatched exceptions, unparsed audit items, and timestamped decision trails ready for your firm's filing software.

Transparent Scoring: The Confidence Bands

No black-box hallucinations. Recon uses an explainable 0–100 weighted composite score with strict hard gates.

Score ≥ 95 · Auto-Suggest

97%+ Measured Precision

Exact reference tokens, identical amount, matching date windows. Pre-focused for instant Enter acceptance. Still requires human verification.

Score 60–94 · Review Band

Human Review Queue

Fuzzy reference matches, narration similarities, or bounded Tier 4 subset-sum groups (1 bank entry vs 2–4 ledger lines). Full score breakdown displayed.

Score < 60 · Unmatched

Exception Bucket

Rows where hard gates failed (direction mismatch, out-of-window dates) or narration similarity is inadequate. Never guessed or auto-filed.

What Recon does not catch (by design): Screenshot or scanned-only PDFs (refused in v1 rather than guessing), entries outside configured date/amount windows, parties whose narration and reference both change completely, or entries requiring discretionary accounting judgments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know the matching is accurate?

You test it side-by-side during your 30-day Shadow Month. We run Recon alongside your firm's existing manual workflow and generate an empirical accuracy report showing exact precision, recall, and time saved.

What if it breaks on deadline day (the 19th)?

Recon is an upstream prep layer, not your filing ledger. If a service issue occurs, your raw files and previous exports remain intact, and your team can revert to manual entry with zero data hostage risk.

Does Tally already do this?

No. Tally is an accounting and filing platform; it records what you type. Recon runs before Tally, ingests the messy bank PDF, scores candidates, and produces the clean reconciliation schedule for Tally entry.

Will junior articles actually use it?

Yes. Juniors adopt tools that eliminate typing. Recon is built keyboard-first (J/K, Enter, Tab) and sustains 40 decisions a minute with zero mouse clicks needed.
